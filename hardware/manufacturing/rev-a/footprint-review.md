## Capacitor sourcing and derating

C6 is an actual orderable 0603 10µF part: [Murata GRM188Z71A106KA73D](https://www.murata.com/products/productdetail?partno=GRM188Z71A106KA73%23),10 V,±10%,X7R(Murata),1.6±0.2×0.8±0.2×0.8±0.2 mm. [Detailed primary specification](https://search.murata.co.jp/Ceramy/image/img/A01X/G101/ENG/GRM188Z71A106KA73-01A.pdf). This preserves value, package and stated dielectric; note the maker's X7R(Murata) temperature-characteristic condition is50% rated voltage. It is not a guarantee of10µF effective capacitance at1.65 V. DC bias, aging and tolerance reduce capacitance. No measured or manufacturer-guaranteed effective minimum at1.65 V has been established here. Retrieve a current SimSurfing bias curve or measure a sample for final noise/settling qualification. Divider Thevenin resistance5kΩ gives nominal3.18 Hz reference pole; if capacitance halves the pole doubles to6.37 Hz. This affects reference filtering/startup, not the DC divider ratio. No value/package substitution was made.

C1/C2/C9/C11 use KEMET C0603C105K4RACTU, 16 V X7R 1µF. The [manufacturer specsheet](https://search.kemet.com/download/specsheet/C0603C105K4RACTU) was downloaded directly and its page 2 DC-bias plot visually inspected on 2026-09-29: approximately 4% capacitance loss at 3.3 V and 8% at 5 V (graph read, typical simulation, not guaranteed limits). Combining those estimates with -10% initial tolerance and -15% temperature change gives 1µF ×0.96×0.90×0.85 ≈0.734µF at 3.3 V, and 1µF ×0.92×0.90×0.85 ≈0.704µF at 5 V, before aging. These are conservative screening estimates using a typical bias curve, not a worst-case vendor guarantee. They provide plausible margin over TI's 0.47µF effective output minimum (TPS7A20 datasheet p5). The input does not set LDO stability but bypassing is recommended for source-impedance/transient control (section 7.1.2). The KEMET sheet states 3% capacitance loss per decade-hour aging; check component age and effective C2 capacitance in qualification rather than treating the typical calculation as a lifetime guarantee.

It confirms C0603C102J5GACTU: 1000pF, ±5%, 50V, C0G, 0603/1608. No part substitution was required. All passive primary specifications are now resolved.
